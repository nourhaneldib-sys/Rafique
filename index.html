Rafiqرفيق
Prototype. texts arranged by the chapters of Imam an-Nawawi's Riyad as-Salihin, including his chapters on character flaws and his Book of Remembrance: the ayat he quotes at the head of each chapter, and the hadith he attributes to Sahih al-Bukhari or Sahih Muslim. Arabic is shown word for word. Quran translation: Marmaduke Pickthall (1930). Tafsir: Al-Mukhtasar (Tafsir Center for Quranic Studies). Background narrations: Sahih al-Bukhari and Sahih Muslim. Word meanings: Al-Muyassar fi Gharib al-Quran. Ibn al-Qayyim: al-Wabil al-Sayyib (Dar al-Hadith, 1999), with Rafiq's working translation pending scholar review. Hadith translations open on sunnah.com. Which chapter suits which answer, and the suggested steps, are Rafiq's and not part of any source. Not yet reviewed by a scholar; for rulings, ask a qualified scholar.
